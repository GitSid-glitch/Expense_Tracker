Unusual expenses

Potential outliers, with context to help you review them.

An anomaly is a non-refund expense that sits well above the usual range after conversion to INR.

If a category has enough history, the app compares the expense against that category's past behavior. Otherwise it falls back to your overall expense history so newer categories can still be reviewed sensibly.

How to read this: “Comparison basis” tells you whether the expense was judged against category history or overall expense history. "Median" is the typical amount in that comparison group, and "Threshold" is the minimum level above which the expense is flagged.
