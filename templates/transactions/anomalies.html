Spending Anomalies

An anomaly is any non-refund expense that sits well above the usual range in INR.

If a category has enough history, the app compares the expense against that category's past behavior. Otherwise it falls back to your overall expense history so newer categories can still be reviewed sensibly.

How to read this table: "Comparison basis" tells you whether the expense was judged against category history or overall expense history. "Median" is the typical amount in that comparison group, and "Threshold" is the minimum level above which the expense is flagged.
